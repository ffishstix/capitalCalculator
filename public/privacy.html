Privacy and browser storage

Updated 2 November 2026

This personal project website is run by Fin West. Contact Fin about the site or your information at finlaywork1@hotmail.com.

Information used

The planner saves your factory plan, price overrides, and guide progress in your browser's local storage so your work and choices are available on a return visit. This information is not sent to the site's database. You can erase it with your browser's site-data controls. Exported plans are files you choose to save on your own device.

Cloudflare hosts the site and processes requests for its pages, images and public data. Requests can include your IP address, browser information and request time. This is used to deliver and protect the site. If you email support, your address and message are used to answer your enquiry. Fin relies on legitimate interests to respond to enquiries and operate a secure site.

Browser storage and cookies

The planner code does not set analytics or advertising cookies. It uses browser local storage to keep the plan and preferences you choose while using the planner. This supports the requested planning service; you can remove it at any time using your browser's site-data controls. Cloudflare Web Analytics was disabled on 2 November 2026, and the live page was checked afterward without its script. Cloudflare may set security cookies when it challenges a request. For example, a successful challenge can set a cf_clearance cookie; Cloudflare says its default lifetime is 30 minutes. These cookies help protect access to the site. Optional analytics, ads or other non-essential storage would require a new assessment and, where required, consent before use.

Sharing and retention

Cloudflare receives site requests as the hosting provider and hosts the public catalogue and market database. Visitor plans are not stored there. Browser storage remains until you clear it. Support messages are kept while needed to deal with the enquiry and any related legal obligation. Cloudflare says its HTTP request and security analytics can be available for at least 31 days; this does not establish the lifetime of every underlying log. Cloudflare and its subprocessors may process request data outside the UK. Its Data Processing Addendum describes safeguards for restricted UK transfers, including the UK Addendum to standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

For access, correction, deletion or objection requests about information you sent by email, contact finlaywork1@hotmail.com. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
