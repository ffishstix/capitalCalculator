Privacy and browser storage

Updated 2 November 2026

This personal project website is run by Fin West. Contact Fin about the site or your information at finlaywork1@hotmail.com.

Information used

The planner saves your factory plan, price overrides, and guide progress in your browser's local storage so your work and choices are available on a return visit. This information is not sent to the site's database. You can erase it with your browser's site-data controls. Exported plans are files you choose to save on your own device.

Cloudflare hosts the site and processes requests for its pages, images and public data. Requests can include your IP address, browser information and request time. This is used to deliver and protect the site. If you email support, your address and message are used to answer your enquiry. The operator's basis for handling support messages and basic site security data is its legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and operating a secure site.

Browser storage and cookies

The planner code does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Its local storage serves the plan and preferences you request. Cloudflare Web Analytics was enabled and its script was found on the live site on 2 November 2026. Its automatic injection has since been disabled in Cloudflare settings, but this change may take time to reach every visitor. Cloudflare may apply security controls at the network edge; its exact cookie and logging settings still need checking. Optional analytics, ads or other non-essential storage would require a fresh assessment and, where required, consent before use.

Sharing and retention

Cloudflare receives site requests as the hosting provider and hosts the public catalogue and market database. Visitor plans are not stored there. Browser storage remains until you clear it. Support messages are kept while needed to deal with the enquiry and any related legal obligation. Cloudflare log retention and any international processing depend on the live account settings and are being verified.

Your rights

For access, correction, deletion or objection requests about information you sent by email, contact finlaywork1@hotmail.com. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cloudflare's detailed logging, retention and transfer settings have not yet been verified. This notice will be updated when those details are confirmed.
